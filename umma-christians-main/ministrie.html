Ministries

Faith in action

Every gift has a purpose. Every believer has a place to serve.

KCF ministries create practical ways for people of every generation to grow in Christ, use their gifts, care for others, and strengthen the Christian witness across Kajiado.

Find Your Place to ServeExplore Ministries

Why we serve

Ministry is love made visible through willing hands and faithful hearts.

Serving Christ by serving people

We believe ministry belongs to the whole body of Christ. KCF helps churches and organisations work together so that gifts are nurtured, leaders are supported, and communities experience the hope of the Gospel in practical ways.

From worship and prayer to mentoring, outreach, family support, and leadership development, every ministry contributes to a stronger and more compassionate fellowship.

Next generation

Children & Youth

Creating safe, joyful spaces where children and young people encounter Christ, discover their identity, build healthy relationships, and develop confidence to lead with integrity.

Women of faith

Women’s Ministry

Strengthening women through prayer, mentorship, friendship, practical support, and opportunities to use their gifts in the Church and wider community.

Men of purpose

Men’s Ministry

Encouraging men to grow as faithful disciples, responsible family members, servant leaders, and positive examples within their churches and communities.

Spirit & truth

Worship & Prayer

Bringing believers together to seek God, celebrate His goodness, intercede for Kajiado, and cultivate worship that is biblical, sincere, and spiritually uplifting.

Love in action

Outreach & Compassion

Sharing the Gospel while responding to real needs through visits, community initiatives, practical care, encouragement, and partnerships that protect dignity.

Equipped to lead

Leadership Development

Supporting pastors, ministry leaders, and emerging leaders with fellowship, shared learning, mentorship, and tools for healthy, faithful, and effective service.

Our call to serve

“Each of you should use whatever gift you have received to serve others.”

1 Peter 4:10

How to get involved

Start with the gifts God has placed in you.

Discover

Identify your gifts, passions, experience, and the needs that move your heart.

Connect

Talk with KCF or your local church leadership about opportunities to participate.

Serve

Join a team, remain teachable, and offer your time with consistency and love.

You are needed

There is room for your contribution.

You do not need a title to make a difference. A listening ear, a willing heart, professional experience, creative ability, prayer, encouragement, or practical help can become a powerful expression of God’s love.
