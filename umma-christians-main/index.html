About KCF

A Christian fellowship built for unity, service, and growth.

Who we are

KCF exists to strengthen the church, support Christian institutions, and create a visible network of worship, discipleship, and service across Kajiado.

Who we are: a fellowship that brings churches, ministries, and Christian organizations together.
Our purpose: to encourage unity, strengthen leadership, and support meaningful Christian service.
