About KCF

Many congregations. One body. A shared calling.

Who we are

KCF creates meaningful connections across the Christian community so that no church, ministry, institution, or leader has to serve alone. Together, we worship, learn, pray, plan, and respond to the needs around us.

Our identity: a Christ-centred family bringing churches, ministries, schools, and Christian organisations together.
Our purpose: to nurture unity, equip leaders, deepen discipleship, and inspire compassionate service.
