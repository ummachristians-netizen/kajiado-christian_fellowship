About Kajiado Christian Fellowship

Stronger together in faith and purpose.

KCF is a Christ-centred family connecting churches, ministries, Christian institutions, and believers across Kajiado County. We create space for people to know one another, grow together, and serve their communities with greater impact.

Our heartbeat

Christ at the centre. People walking together.

We believe unity strengthens the Church and makes the love of Jesus more visible in every community.

Who we are

A fellowship where every Christian community has a place and a voice.

Connected by more than a name

Kajiado Christian Fellowship exists to build genuine relationships across congregations and organisations. We honour the rich diversity of Christian traditions represented in our county while holding firmly to the faith that unites us in Jesus Christ.

Through prayer, worship, discipleship, leadership development, outreach, and shared events, we help members encourage one another and respond together to the spiritual and practical needs of Kajiado.

Our foundation

Rooted in Christ

Jesus Christ shapes our identity and direction. Scripture, prayer, worship, and faithful discipleship guide everything we do.

Our fellowship

Growing in unity

Our witness
