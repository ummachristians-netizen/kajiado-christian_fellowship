Our Vision & Mission

A united Christian community bringing hope and transformation to Kajiado.

We envision churches, ministries, institutions, and believers working side by side—spiritually strong, faithfully led, and actively serving every community.

Our vision

A thriving, united, and Christ-centred fellowship transforming lives across Kajiado.

Our mission

Nurture faith. Equip leaders. Mobilise service.

Our mission is to connect and strengthen the Christian community through prayer, worship, discipleship, leadership development, shared learning, and compassionate outreach.

We help members discover their gifts, grow in spiritual maturity, build healthy partnerships, and respond together to the needs and opportunities within Kajiado County.

What this vision looks like

Healthy churches. Equipped people. Hope-filled communities.

We are working toward a Kajiado where Christian communities are spiritually vibrant, leaders serve with integrity, young people find purpose, families receive support, and churches respond together when communities face difficulty.

Our vision is not measured only by the number of gatherings we hold, but by transformed lives, stronger relationships, faithful leadership, and lasting acts of love that point people to Christ.
